Common ways to use Connect. This list is a living document and will be updated as Connect progresses.

## Make your local Minecraft server public

Once your server is connected to the Connect Network, players can join it even if it runs locally on your PC, without any port forwarding. See [Localhost servers](/guide/localhost).

## Interconnect your Minecraft servers easily

The Connect Network gives every connected server a public, protected address, so a small setup does not need its own internet-facing proxy. Install the [Connect plugin](/guide/connectors/plugin) on your servers, or keep your Gate, Velocity or BungeeCord proxy and connect that instead. [Forwarding and topologies](/guide/topologies) shows which setup fits.

## Advertise your server to more players

Connect is a public place where players browse all connected servers from in-game. Players can join your server directly by your domain, or discover it in the Connect Browser Hub.

## Move players between your servers <Badge type="caution">Planned</Badge>

Today, moving players between servers is done by your own proxy (Gate, Velocity or BungeeCord) behind Connect.

As the server owner, you control all your connected servers and players. Planned public APIs will let you manage your connected endpoints (servers or proxies) and players, for example to send players a message or move them between your servers.

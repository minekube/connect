Common ways to use Connect. This list is a living document and will be updated as Connect progresses.

## Make your local Minecraft server public

Once your server is connected to the Connect Network, players can join it even if it runs locally on your PC, without any port forwarding. See [Localhost servers](/guide/localhost).

## Interconnect your Minecraft servers easily

The Connect Network works as a Proxy-as-a-Service and replaces traditional Minecraft proxies like BungeeCord. You no longer need to configure and maintain a secure proxy yourself. All you need is the [Connect Plugin](/guide/connectors/plugin) installed on your servers.

## Advertise your server to more players

Connect is a public place where players browse all connected servers from in-game. Players can join your server directly by your domain, or discover it in the Connect Browser Hub.

## Move players between your servers without running your own proxy

The Connect Network already works like a global shared proxy that servers and sub-proxies connect to, so you do not need to deploy your own.

As the server owner, you control all your connected servers and players. Public APIs will be available to manage your connected endpoints (servers or proxies) and players, for example to send players a message or move them between your servers.

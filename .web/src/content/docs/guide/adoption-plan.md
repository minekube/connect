---
title: "Adoption plan"
description: "The phased plan for growing the Minekube Connect community: which features and audiences come first, and how adoption is measured."
---

We plan to grow the Connect community in phases, starting with the most important features and audiences.

Adoption is measured by:

- Connect Plugin **installations**
- Servers **linked** to the Connect Network
- Users playing on servers **through** the Connect Network

## Step 1: Friends and localhost servers

The first audience is friends who want to play together on servers hosted on their own computers, and server owners experimenting with Connect.

## Step 2: Grow the creator and player pool

The second audience is creators who want to grow their player base, and players who want to discover new servers.

This step focuses on the Connect Browser: an interactive in-game server list where players can browse, join and review servers with their friends. Gamification, referral and friends systems are key to this step and beyond.

## Step 3: Developers

The third audience is developers who want to extend the Connect Platform and build their own plugins and services on top of supported Connect capabilities.

Connect includes a developer platform and marketplace for Minecraft plugins and services integrated with Connect.
